Printing Tips

Simple tips to help you prepare your files and get better printing results.

🖨️

Check Your File Before Printing

Review your document carefully before sending it for printing. Check the text, images, page size, and layout to avoid unnecessary corrections or reprints.

📄

Choose the Right Size

Make sure you know the required paper or material size before placing your order. Providing the correct size helps ensure that your finished print matches your intended design.

🔍

Use Clear Images

For projects that include photos, logos, or graphics, use clear and high-quality images whenever possible for a sharper final output.
